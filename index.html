About BCNSHS

The Bais City National Science High School (BCNSHS) a premier educational institution in Bais City, Negros Oriental, dedicated to nurturing intellectually gifted students, particularly in the fields of Science and Mathematics.

Our school is committed to providing a specialized curriculum that challenges students and prepares them for higher education and future careers in science and technology. We foster a culture of critical thinking, innovation, and excellence while instilling strong values of leadership and social responsibility.

With highly qualified teachers, state-of-the-art laboratory facilities, and a focus on research, BCNSHS aims to produce responsible, well-rounded, and globally competitive Filipino scientists and leaders.

A Message to Our Scholars

Today, as you step across the threshold of Bais City National Science High School, you are not just entering a school, you are claiming your place in an environment built for excellence, a sanctuary for the curious, and a launchpad for the ambitious. This is where your potential transforms into purpose.

To Our Incoming Grade 7 and 11 Students: Welcome to the Launchpad!

To our newest scholars, the trailblazers of the junior high school program: Welcome home! The moment you passed the rigorous entrance examination, you proved you possess the sharp intellect and unwavering determination needed to thrive in a specialized science high school. BCNSHS is a place that will challenge you, not to break you, but to mold you. Embrace the complexity of the advanced Science and Math curricula. View every difficult problem as an opportunity to sharpen your mind, and every late night of study as an investment in your future. You will discover new passions in our laboratories, forge lasting friendships with like-minded peers, and be guided by teachers who are dedicated not just to teaching subjects, but to mentoring the next generation of Filipino innovators. Your journey in junior high is about building the foundation. Lay it strong, lay it deep, and let curiosity be your constant companion. Remember, you have chosen to Aim High—let the rigor of BCNSHS be the wind beneath your wings.

To Our Incoming Grade 12 Graduates: The Final Ascent to Soaring High!

To our senior high school scholars, the leaders of the student body, and those preparing for the leap into university life: This is your final, critical year. The challenge is immense, but so is the preparation you have received. You have mastered the fundamentals and now stand ready to conquer specialized Senior High School tracks, delve into advanced research, and prepare for the ultimate proving ground: college entrance exams. This year is not just about completing requirements; it is about legacy. Use your knowledge and leadership to mentor your younger counterparts, excel in your Capstone projects or research, and articulate your learned scientific principles with clarity and confidence. Finish strong, leave a mark of excellence, and be ready to Soar High!

To all students, regardless of your grade level, remember the spirit that defines us:
"Aim High, Soar High, Bais City Science High!"
